# Revenue vs Demand: A Menu Pricing Analysis Using Tableau

## Problem Statement
The business observes high sales volume across multiple pizza products but is uncertain whether popularity translates into revenue efficiency.  
This project investigates whether revenue performance is driven by demand, pricing, or menu design.

## Tools Used
- Tableau (Dashboarding & Calculated Fields)
- CSV Data (Sales Transactions)
- GitHub (Project Documentation)

## Dataset Used
- <a href="https://github.com/busariumar/revenue-vs-demand-tableau-project/blob/main/pizza_sales.csv">Pizza Sales</a>

## Key Business Questions
- Do high-selling pizzas generate the most revenue?
- Which category–size combinations truly drive revenue?
- Are some popular items suppressing average order value?
- What pizzas generate the most money per sale?

## Dashboard Overview
The <a href=https://github.com/busariumar/revenue-vs-demand-tableau-project/blob/main/Pizza%20Sale%20Dashboard.jpg>dashboard</a> compares revenue, quantity, and revenue efficiency to identify misleading best-sellers and uncover true revenue drivers.

## Key Insights

- High sales volume does not guarantee high revenue. Several popular pizzas contribute little to total revenue.
- Small pizzas inflate order counts while suppressing average order value.
- Large pizzas account for approximately 45% of total revenue, making size strategy critical.
- Revenue efficiency varies significantly within categories, with some low-volume pizzas generating outsized value.

  ## Business Recommendations
- Promote size upgrades to large pizzas to increase revenue.
- Re-evaluate pricing of small pizzas with high demand but low revenue contribution.
- Protect high revenue-per-unit pizzas even if they sell less frequently.
- Optimize the menu based on category–size performance rather than popularity alone.
